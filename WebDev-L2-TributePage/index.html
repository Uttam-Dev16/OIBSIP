Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam

About Him

Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam was an Indian aerospace scientist and the 11th President of India. He played an important role in India's space and defence programmes and became widely known for his contribution to India's missile and nuclear capabilities.

Born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, Kalam developed a strong interest in science and engineering. He later worked with organisations including DRDO and ISRO, where his work contributed to important national projects.

Beyond science and public service, Kalam was admired for his connection with students and young people. Through his books, speeches and interactions, he encouraged young minds to dream, learn and contribute to the development of the nation.

His life combined scientific work, public service and a strong commitment to education. He continued writing and interacting with students even after his presidency, leaving behind a lasting influence on generations of learners.
